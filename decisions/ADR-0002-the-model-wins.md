## Decision

550,638 deciding entities and weekly ticks are held whatever they cost. **N1, N3, N5 and N6 are
requirements; N2a, N2b and N4 are targets** — measured nightly, trend published, and restated rather than
paid for out of the model. **This records founding decision D1.**

## Why

A performance budget that can stop the project is a budget that will be met by shedding agents or
coarsening cadences, and both are retreats from M1, which is the point of the project. The cheapest way to
hit a tick budget is always to have less model.

Making the target the flexible side removes the incentive permanently, and it does so **before** any
measurement exists to argue about — which is the only time such a rule can be set without appearing to be
a response to a number somebody did not like.

**N3 stays a requirement** because it is a statement about structure and not about speed: a cost that
grows with stock says the design has a pass over the world in it.
