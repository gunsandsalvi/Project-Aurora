# §16.1 rule 4: a `structural` entry names one of the definitional identities. The specification says
# "sixteen" and never lists them; ADR-0013 enumerates them, and another is an ADR.
#
# **Eighteen since ADR-0019.** `BurnInEnsemble` and `BurnInPanel` were added when the burn-in
# correction made the ensemble size a derived figure rather than a declared one, and
# `SpearmanCritical5Percent` was rewritten in the same decision: its "5% at n = 16" was two of the
# numbers ADR-0019 supersedes.
#
# An identity is definitional arithmetic or a closed decision about the shape of the world — never a
# measurement and never a choice about how the economy behaves.
UnitOfAccount                 §5.3, §13.2 — the numéraire S, the unit every nominal figure is counted in
AsymmetryQuadruple            §13.3 — the unique zero-mean unit-variance equally-spaced quadruple
FixedPointOfPublishedIndex    §13.4 — the inflation target, zero, the identity element of a growth rate
SpearmanCriticalGateSize      §15.3, ADR-0019 — the two-sided critical value of Spearman's rho at the gate's ensemble size and per-hypothesis size
BurnInEnsemble                §15.3, ADR-0019 — forty seeds, derived from what B3 needs under the correction
BurnInPanel                   §15.3 — forty-two flagged series, whose four tests are the 168-hypothesis conjunction
RegionCount                   §13.1 — four, and a fifth reopens A4
SectorCount                   §13.1 — seven
ShardCount                    §11, §12.3 — sixty-four, a saved run parameter and not a device property
PeriodsPerYear                §10 — fifty-two, a week being the tick
RunLength                     §2.1 — 1,560 periods, thirty simulated years
RehypothecationDepth          §6.5 — three
IntrinsicQuestionCount        §7.2 — thirteen, and a fourteenth breaks every type until each is decided
RegimeCount                   §7.3 — seven
PositionCount                 §9.4 — twenty-one stable names in the committed period order
ObservationCap                §14 — 2,048 series, under sub-caps that sum to exactly it
LineCap                       §9.5 — 4,096 priced lines, checked at venue registration
JournalRetention              §6.6 — two ticks, below which §9.3's prior-close reads break
